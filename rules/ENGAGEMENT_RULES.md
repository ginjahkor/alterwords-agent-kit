# Engagement rules

How to read your numbers and change what you do. All numbers come from Buffer only (RULE 0).

- **E1. Judge a video at two days old.** Before that the numbers are still moving. Leave posts younger than two days out
  of every average.
- **E2. Compare medians, not single videos.** Use the typical (median) views and watch time of your last 7 days against
  the 7 days before. One video that takes off is a lead, not a pattern.
- **E3. Watch time beats views.** Average watch time divided by video length tells you whether the hook and the middle
  work. Under about 20% of the length means the first seconds lose people: change the hook first.
- **E4. Shares and comments are the strongest signal.** A video with shares or comments shows you what people wanted to
  pass on or argue about. Write down what it had: topic, hook, first frame, length, ending question.
- **E5. Change one thing at a time.** A test changes one thing (a follow line, a trend sound, a hook style, a length)
  and keeps the rest. Write the test down before you start: what changes, on which videos, when you will judge it.
- **E6. Separate the cause.** If the videos in a test also share a topic (for example money stories), you cannot tell
  whether the test or the topic moved the numbers. Run the next one on a different topic before you decide.
- **E7. End on an easy question.** A two-choice question ("Love or strategy?", "Starter or Sports? Comment one.") is
  easier to answer than an open one.
- **E8. Tell the owner what Buffer cannot show:** follower counts, saves, what comments say, and whether a platform is
  limiting a video in some places. Ask the owner to check those in the app on their phone.
- **E9. Report it plainly.** A daily or weekly review gives the numbers, the tests and their state, and the decisions the
  owner has to make. It changes no posts, rules or schedules on its own.
