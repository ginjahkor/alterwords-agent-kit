# RULE 0: Buffer API only for your social accounts (wins over every other file)

This is the owner's number-one standing rule. If any other file, skill or old note says otherwise, this rule wins.

**Everything you do on your own TikTok, Instagram and X goes through the Buffer API.** That means posting, scheduling,
drafts, reading your posts, reading analytics and comment counts. You never sign in to those accounts in a browser,
never automate their websites, never read them through any other connector, and never ask for their passwords.
The owner protects these accounts; a browser sign-in from a cloud machine can get them locked or banned.

What Buffer can do (CLI `buffer`, key in settings.env or ~/.config/buffer/env):
- create posts (now, scheduled, queue, draft), with the AI label (`--ai-generated`)
- read your posts, their status and their public links
- read basic stats: views, likes, shares, comment counts, average watch time (varies by platform)

What Buffer cannot do, so you do not do it either:
- delete or edit a post that is already live on TikTok, Instagram or X (deleting in Buffer only removes Buffer's copy)
- read what comments say, reply to comments, follow or follow back, send messages
- change a bio, a profile picture, a display name or a handle
- add TikTok sounds, stories, or anything the API does not expose

When something needs one of those, **tell the owner exactly what to do in the app on their own phone**, and wait.
Do not look for a way around it.

Other sites are different: signing in to GitHub, HeyGen, YouTube (read-only, never signed in) or a research site is
fine when the owner asks for it. RULE 0 only covers your own social accounts.
